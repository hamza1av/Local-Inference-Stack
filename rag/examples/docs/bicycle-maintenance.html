Bicycle Maintenance Checklist

A short routine that keeps a commuter bike safe and quiet.

Before every ride

  • Squeeze both brake levers: they must stop before touching the handlebar.
  • Check tyre pressure; city tyres usually want 4 to 5 bar.
  • Make sure the lights work.

Chain care

Clean and lube the chain every 300 km or after riding in the rain. Wipe it with a rag, apply one drop of chain oil per roller, wait five minutes and wipe off the excess. Measure chain wear with a chain checker; replace the chain at 0.5% stretch to save the cassette.

Brake pads

Rim brake pads have wear grooves: replace them when the grooves are gone. Disc brake pads should be replaced when less than 1 mm of pad material is left.
